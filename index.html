about

Monitoring Shouldn't Be Hard

Cloudx247 exists because every business deserves to know when their systems go down — without hiring a DevOps team.

Our Philosophy 01

Most startups and SMBs know monitoring is important, but implementing it properly takes time, expertise, and ongoing costs they can't justify. We bridge that gap. We're not a managed service — we're a setup service. We implement, deliver, and get out of your way.

Why Monitoring Matters 02

Downtime costs money and trust. A 10-minute outage can mean lost revenue, frustrated users, and damaged reputation. Yet many businesses find out about outages from their customers — not their tools. We make sure you're always the first to know.

Our Promise 03

No lock-in, no upsells, no recurring charges. We pick the best monitoring tool for your needs, configure it end-to-end, and hand everything over. The credentials, the dashboards, the alerts — it's all yours. If you ever need changes, come back. But you don't have to.
